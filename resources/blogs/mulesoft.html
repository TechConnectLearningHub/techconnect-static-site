Introduction to MuleSoft

Learn how MuleSoft connects applications, APIs and enterprise systems using a simple API-led architecture.

What is MuleSoft?

MuleSoft is an integration platform that helps different systems, applications and services communicate with each other. It is widely used in enterprises to connect cloud systems, databases, APIs and business applications.

MuleSoft API-Led Architecture

MuleSoft follows a three-layer API-led connectivity model: Experience APIs, Process APIs and System APIs. This approach helps organisations build reusable, modular and scalable integrations.

Why Do Companies Use MuleSoft?

Modern businesses use many different systems. Without integration, data becomes disconnected and processes slow down. MuleSoft helps these systems share information securely and consistently through APIs.

  • Connects cloud and on‑premise systems
  • Improves data flow between applications
  • Reduces manual work through automation
  • Supports reusable and scalable integrations

Real‑World Example

In an online shopping system, MuleSoft can connect customer data, inventory, payments and shipping systems. When a customer places an order, MuleSoft ensures each system receives the right information at the right time.
